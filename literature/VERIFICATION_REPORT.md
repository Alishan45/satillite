# Literature Verification and Citation Audit Report

This report documents the verification of all scientific literature cited in the research survey on all-sky satellite data assimilation, prepared for Dr. Juanjuan Liu at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Every paper and digital object identifier (DOI) was audited against official CrossRef metadata, journal landing pages, and institutional repositories.

---

### Summary of audit findings

1. **DOI Integrity**: All 23 citations across the survey and comparison table now link directly to their authentic, official journal publication records. Zero non-resolving or mismatched DOIs remain.
2. **Author and Title Corrections**:
   - Corrected Okamoto (2017) DOI: previously attached to `Geer et al. (2017)`, now correctly assigned to `Kozo Okamoto (2017), QJRMS, DOI: 10.1002/qj.3022`.
   - Corrected Geer et al. (2018) DOI: `10.1002/qj.3202` in QJRMS. The paper covers operational all-sky microwave temperature and humidity assimilation across global centres.
   - Corrected Saunders et al. (2018): previously cited with an incorrect BAMS DOI pointing to a precipitation paper (`10.1175/BAMS-D-17-0107.1`); now verified as the official RTTOV-12 reference in *Geoscientific Model Development* (`DOI: 10.5194/gmd-11-2717-2018`).
   - Corrected Honda et al. (2018): previously cited with an invalid DOI; now verified as `Monthly Weather Review`, `DOI: 10.1175/MWR-D-16-0357.1`.
   - Corrected Minamide and Zhang (2017): previously cited with an invalid DOI; now verified as `Monthly Weather Review`, `DOI: 10.1175/MWR-D-16-0257.1`.
   - Corrected Ukkonen et al. (2020): co-author list verified as *Peter Ukkonen, Robert Pincus, Robin J. Hogan, Kristian Pagh Nielsen, and Eigil Kaas* in *Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems* (`DOI: 10.1029/2020MS002226`).
   - Corrected Hilburn et al. (2021): verified as *Kyle A. Hilburn, Imme Ebert-Uphoff, and Steven D. Miller* in *Journal of Applied Meteorology and Climatology* (`DOI: 10.1175/JAMC-D-20-0084.1`) with open preprint on arXiv (`2004.07906`).
   - Added Dr. Juanjuan Liu's co-authored paper on FY-4A AGRI clear-sky radiance assimilation: *Lan Xu, Wei Cheng, Zhongren Deng, Juanjuan Liu, Bin Wang, Bin Lu, Shudong Wang, and Li Dong (2023), Advances in Atmospheric Sciences*, `DOI: 10.1007/s00376-022-1380-3`.
